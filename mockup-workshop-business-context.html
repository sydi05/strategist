Zone 1 · programme_trigger
REQUIRED
"What forced this conversation now?"
Vendor X end-of-life in 18 months
Board mandate to consolidate
New CEO 90-day plan
Convention: yellow stickies, one root cause per sticky. Min 1 · Max 6.
Zone 2 · enterprise_priorities
REQUIRED
"Which of your enterprise strategic priorities does this programme advance?"
Reduce operating cost by FY28
Lift cross-brand customer value
Modernise digital commerce
Convention: blue stickies, verbatim priority statement, one per sticky. Optional reference link. Min 1 · Max 5.
Zone 3 · stated_intent
REQUIRED
"In one sentence, what is this programme trying to do?"
"We need to move to Commerce Cloud."
Convention: large pink sticky, one statement only. If room produces more than one, capture each as a sub-sticky and flag for strategist to resolve. Min 1 · Max 3.
Zone 4 · success_signal
REQUIRED
"What would change, observably, if this programme worked?"
35% reduction in run cost
Cross-brand purchases 4% → 9%
Cart abandonment under 61%
Convention: green stickies, one signal per sticky. Prefer measurable phrasing. Min 1 · Max 4.
Zone 5 · in_scope
REQUIRED
"Which capability areas does this programme address?"
Commerce experience
Customer identity
Catalogue management
Checkout & payments
Convention: green stickies, one capability area per sticky. Customer's vocabulary preserved. Min 1.
Zone 6 · out_of_scope
REQUIRED
"What's explicitly NOT in scope, even though someone in the room might assume it is?"
Store POS systems
Supply chain & logistics
Brand merchandising autonomy
Convention: red stickies, one exclusion per sticky. Name the assumption being ruled out. Min 1.
Zone 7 · sponsor
REQUIRED
"Who owns this programme at the executive level?"
Sarah Park
CEO
sarah.park@marlinretail.com.au
Convention: one purple sticky, format "Name | Role | Email". Min 1 · Max 1 (more than one means there's no sponsor).
Zone 8 · parking_lot
OPTIONAL
Items the facilitator defers for later.
Pricing strategy?
Loyalty programme
Convention: orange stickies. Not extracted by the plugin, but visible to the agent for context.
Sticky colour conventions
Yellow — root causes / triggers
Blue — enterprise priorities
Pink — stated intent (one only)
Green — success signals + scope_in
Red — scope_out exclusions
Purple — sponsor
Orange — parking lot (not extracted)
This is a visual reference for the FigJam frame Mladen builds in FigJam itself. Stickies shown are illustrative — populated with Marlin Retail Group example content. Validation rules, extraction contract, and zone semantics are in figjam-plugin-spec.md §3.
