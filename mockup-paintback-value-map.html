Paint-back conventions
Three connected frames
Chain + drops (top) · value tree (middle) · hypothesis register (bottom). The chain+drops frame is the structural backbone; the tree is the diagnosis; the register is the validation contract. Sponsors read top to bottom.
Chain + drops is read-mostly
Drops are derived from drivers and the activities they touch. Editing drops on the paint-back creates inconsistency — drop edits flow through the Value Drivers workshop, not the paint-back. Sponsors comment on this frame via the comments zone instead.
Contested metrics flagged inline
When framing_notes.contested_metrics references a metric, the painted metric carries a "contested" badge. Sponsors see the disagreement without opening separate framing notes.
TBD-baseline highlighted
Baselines flagged TBD render with a yellow background so sponsors know which numbers need sourcing. Reduces "is this real?" questions during review.
Confidence colour-coded
Hypothesis confidence rendered in green (high) / orange (medium) / red (low). Low-confidence hypotheses are visible at a glance — they're the conversation starters.
