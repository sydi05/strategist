MARLIN RETAIL GROUP
Program Vision & Strategic Alignment
22 April 2026 · v2
Programme intent
Marlin's digital commerce footprint is the residue of acquisition, not strategy. The board's mandate is to consolidate before vendor end-of-life forces a rushed migration on the worst possible terms. The opportunity is to standardise the 80% of commerce mechanics that are common across the four brands — checkout, payments, fulfilment, product catalogue, customer identity — while preserving the brand-specific 20% the brand teams correctly defend.
Why now
Vendor X end-of-life in 18 months; board mandate to consolidate before FY27; new CEO 90-day plan requires a credible commerce story.
In scope
· Commerce experience
· Customer identity
· Catalogue management
· Checkout & payments
Out of scope
· Store POS systems
· Supply chain & logistics
· Brand merchandising autonomy
PRIORITY 1
Reduce operating cost by FY28
Consolidation removes the run-cost duplication of four parallel commerce stacks.
PRIORITY 2
Lift cross-brand customer value
Shared identity unlocks cross-brand purchase patterns hidden by today's four-stack split.
PRIORITY 3
Modernise digital commerce
Replatform on Commerce Cloud before vendor EOL forces a rushed migration.
Marlin runs one platform behind four brand experiences within 18 months — with measurable run-cost reduction, lifted cross-brand purchase rate, and checkout standardisation across all brands.

Paint-back conventions
Read-mostly
The painted frame mirrors slide 2 of the Vision deck. Sponsors don't edit the frame directly — they mark it up with comment stickies in the dedicated comments zone below.
Round-trip via comments
The plugin extracts comment stickies from the comments zone and passes them to the agent. The agent decides whether to revise (v2) or respond. Comment stickies don't mutate the schema directly.
Theming via design.md
Colours, customer name, sponsor footer pull from the engagement's design.md. The frame structure is constant; the styling varies per customer.
Versioning
Each paint-back is tagged with the schema version it was rendered from. When the agent publishes v3, the previous paint-back frame is archived and a new one is painted.
