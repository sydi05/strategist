🔒 LOAD-BEARING Political signals are blackboard-only. Zone 7 is the only zone in the entire Strategist workflow that is captured but never rendered. The facilitator collects political content in a private working zone; the plugin writes it to framing_notes.political_signals on the blackboard. The render functions for the Stakeholder paint-back and the Stakeholder deck both hard-strip this field before producing any visible artefact. Politics cannot leak through screenshots, shares, exports, or sponsor previews. This is enforced at render time, not by convention.
