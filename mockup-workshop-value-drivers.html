Sticky conventions inside the tree: Blue = objectives (verbatim priority statement) · Green = drivers · Yellow rows = metrics (rendered here as compact rows; in FigJam these are yellow stickies with format "metric | unit"). Pink rows = baseline · Orange = target · Purple = horizon.

Grouping is load-bearing. Drivers must sit visually under their objective. Metrics must sit visually under their driver. The plugin reads the physical grouping — it cannot guess from sticky text alone.

Key pattern — physical grouping carries semantics. This is the first workshop frame where stickies have parent-child relationships. The plugin extraction reads the spatial grouping — drivers sit under their objective, metrics sit under their driver. The facilitator must enforce the layout discipline. If the room moves stickies around the canvas freely, the extraction will return ungrouped content and validation will fail.
